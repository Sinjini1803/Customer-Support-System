# Delivery, Shipping, and Tracking Policy & FAQ

## Shipping Methods & Delivery Timelines
- Standard Shipping: Delivers within 3 to 5 business days.
- Priority Expedited Shipping: Delivers within 1 to 2 business days. Guaranteed next-day dispatch.
- Overnight Express: Next business day delivery if ordered before 2:00 PM EST.

## Delayed Orders & Tracking Troubleshooting
### What should an agent do if a customer package is delayed?
1. Check Courier Checkpoint: Verify carrier tracking via carrier API or dispatch tracking dashboard.
2. In Transit Without Scans (under 3 days): Reassure the customer that transit delays can occur during high volume. Provide the latest carrier scan timestamp.
3. In Transit Without Scans (over 3 days past guaranteed date):
   - Immediately contact the priority dispatch team to file a carrier trace.
   - Offer an immediate waiver or full refund of the shipping fee paid.
   - For high urgency (e.g. gifts, medical/work essentials), arrange a complimentary replacement shipment via Overnight Express at zero cost.

## Missing or Lost Packages
- If tracking shows "Delivered" but the customer has not received it:
  - Ask customer to check around porch, parcel lockers, and with neighbors.
  - If still missing after 24 hours of delivery scan, declare the parcel as lost in transit.
  - Automatically approve either:
    a) A free replacement dispatched with signature confirmation, or
    b) A 100% full refund back to the original payment method.

## Address Corrections Prior to Dispatch
- Customers can update their delivery address within 60 minutes of placing an order.
- Once an order status is "Dispatched / Shipped", address changes cannot be made directly; a carrier interception request must be submitted with a $10 re-routing fee waived for first-time issues.
