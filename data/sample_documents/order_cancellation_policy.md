# Order Cancellation Policy & Guidelines

## Order Cancellation Window
- Orders in "Processing / Pending" status can be cancelled immediately at no cost.
- The standard cancellation window is within 2 hours of order placement.
- If an order has not been picked or packed in the fulfillment warehouse, the support agent can execute an instant cancellation from the admin portal.

## Accidental Duplicate Purchases
- When a customer reports an accidental double purchase (e.g., clicking submit twice or network lag during checkout):
  1. Identify the duplicate order ID.
  2. Confirm both orders contain identical items and timestamps within 15 minutes.
  3. Cancel the secondary order immediately.
  4. Ensure that any pre-authorization holds on the customer's card are released immediately.
  5. Send a formal cancellation confirmation email to the customer's registered email address.

## Cancellations After Shipment
- Once an order status is "Shipped / In Transit", it cannot be intercepted or cancelled directly from the system.
- Troubleshooting steps for the agent:
  - Explain to the customer that the parcel is already with the courier.
  - Instruct the customer to refuse delivery when the courier arrives, which returns the package to the warehouse at no extra fee.
  - Alternatively, email a pre-paid return shipping label once the package arrives so the customer can drop it off at any local drop-box for a 100% full refund upon scan.
