# Customer Service Escalation & Dispute Resolution Policy

## Supervisor Escalation Protocols
- When a customer explicitly demands: "Speak to a manager", "Transfer me to a supervisor", or "Escalate this issue":
  1. De-escalation First Step: Empathize calmly without arguing. "I understand how frustrating this has been for you, and I apologize. I can certainly connect you with a supervisor, or I can personally resolve this right now if you prefer."
  2. If the customer reiterates the supervisor request:
     - Immediately initiate a warm transfer to the Tier 2 / Supervisor queue.
     - Provide the customer with the supervisor direct ticket ID (e.g., #ESC-9821).
     - Inform the customer that a senior support specialist will review the conversation history and respond within 15 minutes.

## Legal and Regulatory Threats
- When a customer mentions "consumer court", "BBB (Better Business Bureau)", "lawyer", or "legal action":
  - Maintain a respectful, neutral, and professional tone.
  - Do NOT argue, make legal admissions, or dismiss their concern.
  - Prioritize immediate resolution: if the underlying issue is a pending refund or missing shipment, support agents have discretionary authorization to issue up to a $150 courtesy refund or credit immediately to restore customer goodwill.
  - Log the conversation under the "High Priority Dispute" tag.

## Service Recovery Credits & Goodwill Vouchers
- For repeated service failures, severe delays (> 7 business days), or mishandled tickets:
  - Agent can issue a $20 goodwill credit or a 25% discount voucher for future orders.
  - Escalate ticket to customer experience management for internal quality review.
