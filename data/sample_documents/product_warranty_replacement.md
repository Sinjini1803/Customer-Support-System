# Product Replacement, Warranty & Damaged Items Policy

## Damaged or Shattered Items Upon Arrival
- If a product arrives broken, cracked, defective, or shattered in transit:
  1. Express immediate empathy and apologize for the inconvenience.
  2. The customer is NOT required to return broken glass or hazardous damaged products.
  3. Support agents are empowered to issue an immediate complimentary replacement order with expedited priority shipping.
  4. If the item is out of stock, issue an immediate 100% full refund including all original shipping and tax charges.

## Defective Products & 1-Year Limited Warranty
- All hardware and electronic products carry a 1-year limited warranty against manufacturer defects.
- Troubleshooting steps before replacement:
  - Verify power source, cable connections, and perform a factory reset (hold power button for 10 seconds).
  - If the hardware remains unresponsive, initiate a warranty RMA (Return Merchandise Authorization).
  - Issue a prepaid shipping label for the defective unit.
  - Dispatch a brand-new replacement unit within 24 hours of RMA creation.

## Size or Model Exchanges
- Customers may exchange apparel or accessories for a different size or color within 30 days of delivery.
- Return shipping for size exchanges is completely free.
- The replacement size is reserved in warehouse inventory immediately upon creation of the exchange request.
