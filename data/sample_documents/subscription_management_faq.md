# Subscription Management & Billing Policy

## Subscription Plans & Renewal Cycles
- Monthly Plan: Billed on the same calendar day each month. Can be cancelled anytime before next renewal date.
- Annual Plan: Billed annually at a 20% discount. Renewal notification sent via email 14 days prior to billing.

## Accidental Double Subscription Charges
- If a customer was charged twice for a subscription renewal:
  1. Inspect the billing transaction IDs in the payment portal.
  2. If duplicate charges were triggered, verify whether one is an un-settled authorization hold.
  3. If duplicate settlements occurred, immediately process a reversal of the secondary license fee.
  4. Provide the customer with the reversal transaction ARN (Acquirer Reference Number) for their bank records.

## Subscription Cancellation & Refund Rules
- Monthly Subscriptions: Access continues until the end of the current paid billing cycle. No pro-rated refunds for partial months.
- Annual Subscriptions: Full refund available within 14 days of initial purchase or auto-renewal date. After 14 days, cancellation prevents future renewals.
- Pausing Subscription: Customers can pause their subscription for up to 90 days without losing saved configurations or data.
